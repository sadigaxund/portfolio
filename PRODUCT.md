# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro with React islands, TypeScript, and Tailwind v4, with static output. `my-you-eye` is the first choice for components.

## Users

The primary user is a recruiter or hiring manager who received this URL in a job application. They scan quickly and decide whether to read further.

Secondary users are engineers checking the work, and people who want to know the owner personally.

## Product Purpose

This is Sadig Akhund's portfolio, sent as a link with job applications. Its job is to show the owner's background, what they have built, and why they fit the role.

Success means a recruiter finds the role, experience, and a way to make contact without learning how the site works.

## Positioning

The owner is a software and data engineer whose open-source work is public and inspectable. A visitor can follow a claim on the page to the running tool, repository, or release.

Navigation is one line running down the page. Forks in the line let a visitor choose a path.

## Operating Context

A visitor usually arrives from an application tracker, an email, or LinkedIn, on a work laptop or a phone. A resume PDF is usually open in another tab, so the site should add to it and never only repeat it.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Scrolling alone must reach the professional content. The personal and projects paths are choices, and a visitor who skips them misses nothing a recruiter requires.
- Every node has a real URL, and a deep link opens directly.
- Sections are blog, projects, career, certifications, education, and personal.
- Hosting is decided separately, and the build stays static.

## Brand Commitments

- The site is professional first.
- Consistency is the top priority. The same role looks the same everywhere unless a recorded reason says otherwise.
- The design stays clean and restrained, and avoids common AI-generated patterns.
- Copy is short and concrete, with no long story-like prose.

## Product Principles

1. A recruiter gets the answer first. Role, experience, and contact never sit behind a choice.
2. Every claim links to evidence where public evidence exists.
3. The line earns its place by making movement clearer. It never delays content.
4. Exploring is a reward and never a requirement.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Keyboard, screen reader, and reduced-motion visitors reach all content.
